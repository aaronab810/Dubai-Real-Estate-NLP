## Overall assessment

The project has a defensible research direction: use NLP to measure Dubai property discussion and compare its attention and sentiment with DLD activity. Peer-reviewed work supports these research concepts. It does not automatically validate our dataset, five chosen groups, MiniLM settings, 25-group reduction, near-duplicate threshold, recovery rules or predictive claims.

The most immediate weakness was inconsistent corpus provenance. The saved topic notebook loaded `final_pipeline_outputs/master_processed.csv` and appended Reddit exports, whereas validation was drawn from `final_pipeline_outputs/master_processed_analysis_clean.parquet`. The latter contains 47,748 records; four remaining AutoModerator records are excluded from the current refit, leaving 47,744. The old topic corpus had 87,574 records. These are different corpus versions, not consecutive steps that can be subtracted and called 'removed by cleaning'. Some clean texts are absent from the old corpus.

The canonical topic notebook now defaults to the final clean export, with no raw Reddit append. The historical append path is an explicit opt-in. New model artifacts use a separate version directory. The executed model's counts, parameters and hashes are in [the refit manifest](../analysis/refit_2026_09_07/refit_manifest.json).

The actual fit assigned 23,261 records to 274 topics and retained 24,483 records as topic -1 (51.28%). The complete model reload was verified. No 25-group reduction or historical SMDI mapping was applied. The model and portable ZIP are local; the attempted Drive upload was blocked by automatic approval review, so no successful Drive upload is claimed.

## What is still lacking, in priority order

1. **A frozen analysis corpus and complete retention ledger.** The refit fixes the input mismatch and excludes the known moderator account. It does not rerun all earlier retrieval, relevance, listing and human-repeat decisions. Each removal needs source record IDs, an exclusive reason, a retained count, and a version/hash. A final clean file name alone does not prove all records are suitable.
2. **Independent validation beyond already-retained records.** The 400 labels assess domain relevance, genuine discourse, promotion and low information. They are not labels of BERTopic themes or sentiment. Evaluate filtered-out material and topic -1 separately, using a stratified sample across platform, category, length and time. Tune on development records and evaluate on untouched records. Report class-specific precision/recall, uncertainty and the sampling design; weight stratified estimates if claiming corpus-wide performance.
3. **Fresh topic and grouping validation.** Inspect representative and random posts, not keywords alone. Check coherence, redundant topics, largest-group share, outlier content, and sensitivity to seeds/parameters. Compare the present UMAP/HDBSCAN baseline with two bounded alternatives: a justified distance cut on non-outlier topic centroids, and HDBSCAN parameter sensitivity on the same fixed embeddings. A distance cut groups existing topics; it does not itself recover outlier posts. Balanced groups are a diagnostic, not a requirement that overrides meaning.
4. **A sentiment or expectation measure tied to the RQ.** Topic frequency measures attention only. Add a small, independently labelled Dubai property sample before choosing a sentiment classifier. Distinguish the target (prices, affordability, developer service, tenancy law) and expressed direction. 'Rents are rising' can mean negative affordability but a positive landlord return. An expectation label ('prices will rise/fall/uncertain') adds a substantive NLP task; a retrospective complaint is different. Keep sentiment separate from attention initially.
5. **An explicit measurement definition.** Define the observational unit as a collected record, separating X posts, Reddit posts and comments. Weekly attention is a count; theme share is the theme count divided by all eligible property-discussion records on that platform that week. Missing collection coverage is not zero attention. Do not call counts investor sentiment, demand, participation in transactions, or representativeness of Dubai residents. Decide whether high-volume Reddit threads should count repeatedly and show thread/author sensitivity summaries.
6. **A constrained temporal comparison.** Use the actual common DLD/social coverage. Predefine the DLD outcome and filters: sale transaction count for the relevant RQ; mortgage counts and off-plan/ready comparisons are separate outcomes with explicit coding. Do not substitute monetary transaction value for number of transactions. Record week boundaries, date timezone, missing days and incomplete boundary weeks. Test a very small set of predeclared leads, account for prior DLD activity/calendar effects where feasible, and report sensitivity to influential weeks and repeated testing. Refit topics on training periods only if making an out-of-sample forecasting claim.
7. **Scope clarified by Aaron after this review:** the current paper covers X, Reddit and DLD. Facebook and listing-platform comparisons belong to a later paper and are not missing requirements for this paper. Preserve the professors' original RQs as historical context while stating the present paper's scope explicitly. Any future spatial analysis would also require validated location extraction and adequate counts per community.

Four months is a fixed constraint, not a reason to demand unavailable years. Treat this as an exploratory study of the observed period. Weekly aggregation yields roughly 17 weeks before boundary handling, while monthly aggregation provides only four values. Daily analysis can be a sensitivity analysis if collection coverage and DLD calendar effects are understood; it does not turn correlated days into independent observations or create additional economic cycles. A small number of hypotheses and transparent uncertainty are more defensible than a large predictive model here. There is no universal minimum number of observations that guarantees validity.

## Deduplication policy

Keep duplicate ingestion of the same platform/record ID separate from repeated human messages. Remove known automated moderation from substantive discussion, including its first copy. For topic discovery, compare a version with repeated templates collapsed or downweighted while preserving links back to every original record. For attention, legitimate repetitions across different authors or later weeks can be real activity: use a same-author-per-week cap as a sensitivity series instead of treating all four-month repetition as erroneous ingestion.

The existing final-clean export already applies author-aware exact and near-duplicate rules. The current refit preserves those human-message decisions, so removed messages cannot be recovered from that export alone. Unknown or deleted usernames must not be pooled as one actual author. Near-duplicate normalization should preserve negation, numbers, dates and locations; evaluate the current 0.98 rule on labelled pairs before treating it as established policy. These are project-specific recommendations motivated by the research, not claims that a paper prescribes this exact policy.

Each has 400 rows. All parsed values match the previous downloaded versions. This does not prove the user has not updated another copy. The updated location has been requested; no claim is made to have verified an unseen updated pair.

Reconciliation means deciding how to use a post when the two research assistants disagree. Example: Aaron labels a message 'promotion'; Prajwal labels it 'discussion'. Both read the same message and relevant parent context, consult the written definition, and add a separate final decision plus a short reason. Preserve both original labels. If the case remains ambiguous, mark it unresolved or ask an agreed adjudicator; there is no two-person majority to break a tie.

You do not need to force agreement to calculate Cohen's kappa: kappa describes agreement between the original independent labels. If you later discuss disagreements, the resulting consensus labels can support a final reference set, but kappa calculated after making the answers agree no longer measures independent agreement. The professors are not assumed to have adjudicated anything. The accessible current pair still has 57 records with at least one disagreement; this number must be rechecked if the updated versions differ.

## Answers to the three annotations

**Impact of the different input files:** model training and validation described different populations. Appended records could bypass later promotion, low-information and repeat filters, influencing topic centres, outlier assignments and weekly counts. Consequently, the old validation results do not automatically validate the complete old topic corpus. The refit uses the final cleaned input; old/new counts should be reported as different versions, not one simple subtraction-based cleaning flow.

**What were the strict and broader rules?** The strict candidate bucket requires a property keyword in the message itself, Dubai/UAE context (in the message, available parent, or relevant subreddit), and at least 12 words; identified automation, promotion and non-property rental ambiguities take precedence. Examples of property words are apartment, rent, tenant, landlord, mortgage, Ejari and off-plan. The broader set adds short property messages and replies whose property connection appears only in the parent post. These are keyword/context heuristics, not a trained relevance classifier. The earlier 47–50% strict recall and 92–94% broad recall came from 178 matches to the existing human-labelled sample, not a representative test of every outlier. General geopolitics still requires a property link.

**What did 'organising review' mean?** Put uncertain messages into understandable categories so a small sample from each can be inspected with the right context: direct property discussion, short messages, context-dependent replies, possible promotions and uncertain material. It does not mean asking you to read tens of thousands of posts. The purpose is to discover where a recovery method fails, validate it, and then process the full corpus with measured error rates. Category membership alone is not a final keep/delete decision.
