# Reading the group-versus-market graphs

The PDF contains 11 comparisons covering ten broad groups: developers/projects has separate off-plan and ready panels. Each page shows X on the left and Reddit on the right. Blue is the group's share of all retained platform records that week; dashed orange is the stated DLD outcome. Each line has its own average subtracted and is divided by its own sample standard deviation. Zero means that series' average, not zero posts or sales. A value of one means one standard deviation above that average. The lines compare movement, not absolute amounts. They cover 16 complete Monday-Sunday weeks, 5 January-26 April 2026.

The numerical summaries below describe these same graphs. They do not add new primary tests. The primary family is G1/median amount, G3/off-plan, G3/ready and G9/sales on each platform. The other six contextual groups lack a directly corresponding administrative outcome; their plots must not be interpreted as tests of rents, financing, regulation or living conditions. G6/sales is exploratory. Topic -1 remains in each platform denominator but contributes no category count. Independent category validation is pending.


## G1: Property Market & Pricing / Median sale amount

Primary: registered transaction size, not a price index.

X: same-week Pearson r = -0.28; correlation of weekly changes = -0.32.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = -0.49; correlation of weekly changes = -0.01.

Reddit pricing discussion is higher in some weeks with lower median amounts, but this relationship nearly disappears for weekly changes. Median registered amount also changes with the mix of properties sold; it is not a constant-quality price index.


## G2: Property Investment & Finance / Median sale amount

Context only: does not measure investment returns or financing conditions.

X: same-week Pearson r = -0.19; correlation of weekly changes = -0.31.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = 0.10; correlation of weekly changes = -0.09.

This is contextual. Median sale amount does not measure investment returns, credit access or mortgage conditions. The plot shows timing alongside transaction size only.


## G3: Developers & Property Projects / Off-plan sales registrations

Primary: discussion of developers versus off-plan registration activity.

X: same-week Pearson r = 0.25; correlation of weekly changes = -0.16.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = 0.42; correlation of weekly changes = 0.22.

The broad developer group follows ready-sale activity more closely on Reddit than on X. Its off-plan comparison is weaker. Narrowing the label to off-plan/delivery greatly weakens both Reddit comparisons, so the broad pattern should not be described as a delivery-specific result.

The broad developer group follows ready-sale activity more closely on Reddit than on X. Its off-plan comparison is weaker. Narrowing the label to off-plan/delivery greatly weakens both Reddit comparisons, so the broad pattern should not be described as a delivery-specific result.


## G4: Property Locations & Characteristics / Sales registrations

Context only: aggregate registrations do not validate location-specific discourse.

X: same-week Pearson r = 0.12; correlation of weekly changes = 0.10.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = 0.69; correlation of weekly changes = 0.52.

This is contextual. Citywide sales counts do not test whether discussion of a particular community matches sales in that community. The available location matching is insufficient for a validated spatial comparison.


## G5: Rental & Tenancy / Sales registrations

Context only: no rental-registration series is available.

X: same-week Pearson r = 0.39; correlation of weekly changes = -0.13.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = 0.05; correlation of weekly changes = 0.06.

This is contextual. These are property sales, not rental contracts or rents. Similar-looking movements would not establish a relationship between tenancy conditions and a corresponding rental outcome.


## G6: Property Transactions & Professional Services / Sales registrations

Exploratory: transaction-related discussion versus sales registration activity.

X: same-week Pearson r = 0.59; correlation of weekly changes = -0.06.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = 0.55; correlation of weekly changes = 0.38.

This exploratory pairing compares transaction/service discussion with sales registration activity. It does not measure service quality or the number of buyers represented by the posts.

## G8: Property Management & Living / Sales registrations

Reddit: same-week Pearson r = -0.10; correlation of weekly changes = -0.30.

This is contextual. Sales activity is not a measure of maintenance, property management or living conditions. The graph is a side-by-side calendar comparison.


## G9: Political, Geopolitical & Social Discourse / Sales registrations

Primary: broad political/social attention versus sales activity, not pure geopolitical risk.

X: same-week Pearson r = -0.01; correlation of weekly changes = 0.22.
Reddit: same-week Pearson r = -0.76; correlation of weekly changes = -0.42.

Reddit political/social discussion is higher during some lower-sales weeks; X has little same-week relationship. Weekly changes and equal thread weighting weaken the Reddit result. This broad category includes social reactions as well as geopolitical content, so it is not a pure geopolitical-risk index.


## G10: Communication, Content & Platform Behaviour / Sales registrations

This is contextual. DLD has no corresponding measure of platform behaviour or communication style. These topics help describe the corpus, but sales counts cannot validate their economic meaning.
