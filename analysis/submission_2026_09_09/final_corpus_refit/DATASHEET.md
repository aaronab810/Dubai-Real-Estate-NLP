# Dataset documentation

**Purpose.** Explore relationships between collected Dubai property discourse and registered sale activity while preserving the provenance of every processing step. Intended users are researchers reproducing or extending this descriptive study, not automated lending, trading or individual profiling systems.

**Sources and coverage.** Saved strict/broad X query snapshots; identified Reddit submissions/comments including r/dubai and property-focused sources; a DLD transaction CSV downloaded 19 June 2026. Source dates cover January–April 2026 for analysis. Capture is nonrandom, query- and platform-dependent, and does not enumerate all relevant conversation. Deleted r/dubai bodies account for 10,458 unscored raw records. Timestamps represent social posting and administrative registration, not aligned decisions.

**Composition.** 88,155 raw social records; 74,303 initially eligible records; 72,239 v2 activity records; 71,239 final v4 activity records and 68,285 unique training texts. Final X=11,787, Reddit submissions=4,450, comments=55,002. Common 16-week counts are X=11,362 and Reddit=56,721. January–April DLD=62,012 distinct sales; common-window=59,229. Private identifiers support source membership and joins; no person-to-registration linkage is attempted.

**Processing.** HTML/URL normalization; X mention removal, hashtags exposed as words and emoji verbalization; target/context property rules; known AutoModerator exclusion; target-only solicitation/contact/reaction rules. A second logged repair follows topic inspection and precedes new market-result reading. Distinct IDs remain separate activity observations; exact text is collapsed only for discovery. Near-family retrieval is approximate and used only as a sensitivity. All six topic fits are trained directly on the final v4 text families. The earlier corpus fit is a sensitivity on the same final population. Earlier project outcomes were known, but current corpus/semantic repairs preceded calculation of the revised associations.

**Annotations.** Original 400-record paired labels are historical cleaning-quality evidence, not independent final-corpus or SMDI validation. All 400 join uniquely; 373 remain in v4. Their promotion/low-information labels reveal residual errors. Original r/dubai 500-post development labels yield F1=0.708 at the saved threshold; they are not held out. New independent category review packets contain no human labels; no rejection or geography validation is claimed. No synthetic label is represented as human.

**Semantic enrichment.** Five candidate overlapping categories: geopolitical risk, valuation/returns, development/delivery, rental/tenancy and property finance. Location is separate. Candidate labels combine explicit target cues with nonnoise topic cue support; noise/unclassified records remain denominators. Category precision/recall is unknown. Alternative fits and cue/threshold sensitivities quantify dependence on operationalization, not accuracy.

**Language and missingness.** X uses English metadata. Reddit English eligibility has not been independently measured. Final non-Latin flags=45, missing submission context=393, missing immediate-parent context=366. Blank/unavailable author IDs are replaced by record-specific keys for weighting; they are not assumed to be one user. No validated generic bot detector was run.

**Administrative semantics.** Remaining repeated sales IDs differ only in nearest-place metadata after 171 full-row duplicates are removed across the source. Substantive sales records agree. Registered value is not a house-price index; registration count is not a physical-unit count. The sales deduplication conclusion is not generalized to mortgages/gifts. Transaction and property area fields are distinct. Case-normalized area names do not establish neighborhood equivalence.

**Limitations.** Nonrepresentative participation, residual advertising, contextual ambiguity, duplicate/thread concentration, sparse category numerators, registration delays, changing denominators and 16-week temporal support. No causal or predictive use is established. Literal place mentions are not author residence or a property's verified DLD area.

**Access, maintenance and rights.** Raw/derived social text remains local and private. The source ZIP contains the paper, figures, tables, bibliography and template, not user text/model files. Licensing/redistribution rights are not inferred from accessibility. Source/output hashes document this snapshot; no automatic refresh or continued collection is scheduled. No ethics approval or consent claim has been invented. Authors must assess institutional and venue obligations before external submission.
